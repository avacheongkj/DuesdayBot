# DuesdayBot Privacy Policy

## 1. Introduction

DuesdayBot ("we," "us," "our," or the "Service") is a Telegram bot that helps you track and manage your to-dues (renewals, subscriptions, expirations, and reminders). This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal data. By using DuesdayBot, you consent to these practices.

## 2. Data Controller

DuesdayBot is operated by Ava Chong. For privacy enquiries, contact: avackj1999@gmail.com

## 3. What Personal Data We Collect

We collect the following:

- **Telegram Account Information:** Your Telegram user ID, username, first and last name
- **To-Due Items:** Item names, due dates, categories (subscription, insurance, passport, credit card, etc.), owner (you/Jon/both), links, and custom notes
- **Reminder Settings:** Lead time (days before reminder), reminder type (single/escalating/weekly), and last reminder date
- **Interaction Data:** Your responses to reminders, button taps (Done/Snooze/Compare), and edits you make
- **Preferences:** Custom reminder schedules and notification settings
- **Consent Data:** Your agreement to this privacy policy and the date you agreed

## 4. Legal Basis for Processing (PDPA Compliance)

We process your data based on:

- **Consent:** You provide consent by using DuesdayBot and explicitly agreeing to data collection via the /start command
- **Contractual Necessity:** Processing is needed to provide the Service
- **Legitimate Interests:** Operating, improving, and securing the Service

Under Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA), you can withdraw consent at any time by using the `/stop` command, which will permanently delete all your data.

## 5. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to:

- Store and manage your to-dues in our database
- Send daily reminder notifications at your chosen time (9am by default)
- Process your responses to reminders (marking as done, snooping, comparing)
- Allow you to edit or delete to-dues
- Improve the Service and fix bugs
- Detect and prevent abuse or security issues
- Comply with legal obligations

## 6. How Your Data is Processed

Your data is processed and handled by:

- **Supabase (Database Provider):** Stores your to-dues securely under a data processing agreement
- **Telegram (Messaging Platform):** Processes and transmits reminder messages; governed by [Telegram's privacy policy](https://telegram.org/privacy)
- **Railway (Hosting Provider):** Runs the bot server; bound by a data processing agreement

We do not share, sell, or trade your data to third parties.

**Data Requests:** We may disclose data if required by law, court order, or government request.

**Business Transfers:** If the Service is sold or merged, data may transfer to the successor (you'll be notified).

## 7. Data Retention

- **Active Data:** Retained whilst your account is active
- **Deleted Data:** Permanently deleted within 30 days of your request
- **Backup Copies:** May persist for up to 90 days for disaster recovery
- **Compliance Data:** Anonymised data may be retained for legal compliance

## 8. Your Rights Under PDPA

You have the right to:

- **Access:** Request a copy of your personal data
- **Correction:** Request correction of inaccurate data
- **Deletion:** Request deletion of your data (right to be forgotten) via the `/stop` command
